FAQs

Answers to common questions.

Clear answers. Total confidence. Everything you need to know before you order.

Contact Our Team
01How long does production take?

05-07 business days for normal production. For a rush fee we can make it within 02-03 business days and ship!

02Can my graphics be etched into the stock chain?

No we don't offer that on our stocks, all graphic work is a vinyl on top of the metal.

03How long to make a fully custom premier chain?

After artwork is approved and the deposit is received (1/2 of the invoice to start production), it is normally 03-04 weeks! Not 12 weeks like other "companies".

04How much do fully custom premier chains cost?

We can email you a price guide because there are lots of variations, but they range from $450 to $1300.

05How much for shipping?

Shipping varies depending on where we are shipping, but it is low and insured. We are located in Ohio and generally ship UPS. For example, UPS Ground may only be $16.99 with insurance. We can also ship internationally with other carriers, just contact us for prices.
